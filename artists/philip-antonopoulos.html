Featured Artist · May 2019

Philip Antonopoulos

22 years on film. The Pen FT is his favorite — 72 frames makes him think less and shoot more. Not necessarily trying to tell a story, just the aesthetic.

Brisbane, Australia · @p_a2018_ · Olympus Pen FT · Various

Why film?

For the non-clinical look. Perfection on digital camera bores me even if I still use them sometimes due to cost of film.

Why half frame?

I love the fact that you can shoot 72 frames, and that it has a certain look. The fact that I get 72 frames makes me think less so I get shots I would not have taken with a normal 35mm camera.

What are you trying to show?

Nothing specific. When I shoot I mainly think of the aesthetic or an interesting subject. I am not necessarily trying to tell a story.

HFC is always looking for half frame photographers doing interesting work.
