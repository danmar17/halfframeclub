Featured Artist · March 2019

Sarah Prins

She felt like a big cheater for all the time spent editing digital images to look like half frames. She went fully analog and never looked back. Triptychs out of diptychs.

The Hague, Netherlands · @missesprins · Olympus Pen EE3 · Ektar 100 / Fujichrome Sensia

Why half frame?

About seven years ago my friend gave me a customized Olympus Pen EE3. He was sure I would love it as he knew I am a profound storyteller. I immediately fell in love with the idea but it took me a while before I literally picked it up.

Why film?

I was manipulating and editing my images to look like half frames. Hashtags led me to halfframeclub and scrolling through all those great images I felt like a big cheater. So I decided to finally take the big step and go fully analog.

What are you trying to show?

On Instagram I compose short stories in series of 3... triptychs out of diptychs.

Follow Sarah Prins at @missesprins on Instagram
