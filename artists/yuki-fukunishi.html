Featured Artist · December 2022

Yuki Fukunishi

Bold contrast and stark composition that makes you feel like you are looking at a slice of something bigger. Like a vertical window peering into another world.

Tokyo, Japan · @Penny3ee · Olympus Pen EE3 · Kodak UltraMAX 400

Why film?

It takes time to see the pictures. It makes me excited. Also when I take photos, the film camera makes me think a lot about the subject composition... the sunlight, clouds and shadows.

Why half frame?

It is compact and easy to carry. Plus, I can take many shots so it is economical to use.

Favorite combo?

I loved the Olympus Pen EE3 as soon as I saw it. I am loyal to Kodak UltraMAX 400. The colors are wonderful.

What are you trying to show?

I want to compose landscape images using portrait framing.
